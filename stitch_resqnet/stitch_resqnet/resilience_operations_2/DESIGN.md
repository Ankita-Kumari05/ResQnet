## Brand & Style

This design system is engineered for emergency command centers, first responders, and citizens navigating high-stress crisis scenarios. The operational philosophy centers on panic mitigation through optical calm, unequivocal structural hierarchy, and rapid situational awareness. The system rejects hyper-saturated alerts, decorative blurs, and ambiguous abstractions in favor of calm confidence, high visual clarity, and deliberate cognitive pacing.

The visual style is **Tactical Modernism**: a synthesis of Swiss grid discipline, mission telemetry, and civil defense utility. The interface communicates stability and authority while maintaining an approachable, humane posture. Surfaces remain predominantly neutral (70–80%), punctuated by trustworthy blue anchors (10–15%) and selective, dual-coded semantic indicators (5–10%) to preserve sensory bandwidth under extreme duress.

## Colors

The palette establishes strict visual equilibrium to prevent cognitive overload. Surfaces are dominated by calm, cool neutrals (`#F8FAFC`, `#FFFFFF`, `#F1F5F9`), while primary blue (`#2563EB`) guides operational flow and active states. Chrome and command headers use deep slate navy (`#1E3A5F` and `#0F172A`) to ground viewport boundaries.

Semantic colors adhere to WCAG AAA contrast ratios against their respective light containers:
- **Critical / Emergency:** `#DC2626` text and borders over `#FEF2F2` background. Reserved strictly for immediate life-safety actions, hazard perimeters, and active alerts.
- **Warning / Heightened Vigilance:** `#D97706` over `#FFFBEB` with `#FDE68A` border for environmental advisories and unverified reports.
- **Operational / Verified:** `#16A34A` over `#F0FDF4` with `#BBF7D0` border for cleared zones, operational assets, and confirmed status.
- **Informational / Telemetry:** `#2563EB` over `#EFF6FF` with `#BFDBFE` border for standard dispatches, situational notices, and system metadata.

Color is never deployed in isolation; every semantic state must pair with an explicit textual label and standardized operational iconography.

## Typography

Typography prioritizes instantaneous scanning and high optical definition under glare or low-light field conditions. **Public Sans** provides neutral, institutional clarity for instructions, narrative content, and structural controls. **JetBrains Mono** is introduced for machine-parsed coordinates, telemetry streams, CAP identifiers, and dynamic numerical counters.

Operational rules:
- Apply `tabular-nums` across all numeric readouts to prevent visual vibration during live SITREP updates.
- Micro-labels and status badges are rendered in uppercase with deliberate tracking (`0.06em` to `0.08em`) to guarantee glyph distinction.
- Body copy line lengths must remain between 45 and 75 characters to prevent cognitive fatigue during extended operational shifts.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built on an 8px rhythmic baseline supported by 4px sub-increments for compact telemetry grids and multi-tier command docks.

### Breakpoints & Layout Adaptations
- **Desktop (>= 1280px):** 12-column fluid grid, 24px gutters, 32px outer safe margin. Employs a tri-panel command console: Left Panel (Incident Ingestion Feed, 360px fixed), Center Panel (Tactical GIS / Live Map, fluid), Right Panel (Unit Allocation & SITREP Actions, 420px fixed).
- **Tablet (768px - 1279px):** 8-column layout, 16px gutters, 24px outer margin. Side-panels transform into dismissible drawer overlays or tabbed bottom sheets.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout, 12px gutters, 16px outer margin. Information reflows into single-column vertical cards. Critical life-safety triggers and emergency callouts occupy sticky bottom positions with a minimum touch envelope of 48x48px.

### Density Tiers
- **Telemetry & Grid Density (8px cell padding):** Applied to asset tables, dispatch manifests, and sensor logs to maximize field of view.
- **Citizen & Incident Advisories (16px to 24px container padding):** Applied to warning notices, triage forms, and guidance steps to prevent erroneous touch inputs.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is established primarily through **Tonal Layering** and **Low-Contrast Outlines** rather than expressive drop shadows. This ensures visual stability and battery-conscious performance on ruggedized mobile tablets.

- **Level 0 (Canvas Base):** Surface neutral `#F8FAFC`. Zero elevation, non-interactive floor.
- **Level 1 (Default Containers & Panels):** Surface card `#FFFFFF` with a crisp 1px solid border in `#E2E8F0`. Flat elevation with zero ambient shadow.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Floating & Flyouts):** Surface `#FFFFFF` with 1px border `#E2E8F0` and subtle ambient shadow: `0 4px 6px -1px rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.05), 0 2px 4px -2px rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.03)`. Used for active dropdown menus, context menus, and docked map controls.
- **Level 3 (Tactical Modals & Urgent Overlays):** Surface `#FFFFFF` over a 60% opacity backdrop scrim (`#0F172A`) with a structural drop shadow: `0 12px 20px -4px rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.12), 0 4px 8px -2px rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.06)`.
- **Life-Safety Overrides (CAP Alerts):** Bypasses elevation tiers using an anchoring 4px solid left indicator (`#DC2626` or `#D97706`), rendering with zero vertical shadow to maintain structural integration with the viewport.

## Shapes

The design system employs **Soft Industrial Precision (Roundedness Level 1)** to strike a balance between rigorous utility and humane reassurance.

- **4px (Default):** Checkboxes, radio indicators, data grid tags, code blocks, and operational counters.
- **6px (Medium):** Standard action buttons, text input fields, operational chips, and segmented controls.
- **8px (Large):** Summary cards, incident advisory banners, modal dialogs, and persistent dockable panels.
- **Full (9999px):** Live radio status pills, beacon dots on tactical maps, and numerical notification badges.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Operational:** Solid `#2563EB` background with `#FFFFFF` text, 6px radius, 40px height (`space-10`). Hover: `#1D4ED8`. Active: `#1E40AF`. Focus: 2px offset ring in `#2563EB`.
- **Secondary / Calm Action:** `#EFF6FF` background, `#2563EB` text, 1px border in `#BFDBFE`. Hover: `#DBEAFE`.
- **Tertiary / Utility:** Transparent background, `#1E293B` text, 1px border in `#E2E8F0`. Hover: `#F1F5F9` background.
- **Emergency Action (SOS / Evacuation):** Minimum 48px height, `#DC2626` background, `#FFFFFF` text, bold `label-badge` uppercase typography. Pairs with an alert icon. Focus ring: 3px solid `#DC2626` offset by 2px.

### Badges & Chips
- Status indicators must be dual-coded with a 12px vector icon and bold alphanumeric label.
- **Height:** 22px; **Padding:** 2px 8px; **Radius:** 4px.
- **Critical / Severe:** `#FEF2F2` background, `#FECACA` border, `#DC2626` text and icon.
- **Warning / Caution:** `#FFFBEB` background, `#FDE68A` border, `#D97706` text and icon.
- **Safe / Resolved:** `#F0FDF4` background, `#BBF7D0` border, `#16A34A` text and icon.
- **Info / Staged:** `#EFF6FF` background, `#BFDBFE` border, `#2563EB` text and icon.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Form Fields:** Height 42px, `#FFFFFF` background, 1px border `#E2E8F0`, 6px radius, `#1E293B` text, `#94A3B8` placeholder. Focus: `#2563EB` border with 0px blur 1px outline.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** 18px size, 4px radius for checkboxes, full circle for radios. Default border: 1.5px solid `#CBD5E1`. Checked state: solid `#2563EB` fill with crisp white glyph.

### Cards & Incident Panels
- Background `#FFFFFF`, 1px solid border `#E2E8F0`, 8px radius. Internal padding spans 16px to 24px.
- Incident cards feature an optional status accent bar along the top or left border (3px solid in semantic status color).

### Alert Banners (CAP Aligned)
- Spans 100% container width with an assertive 4px solid left indicator.
- Internal padding: 12px 16px. Background matches semantic tint (`#FEF2F2`, `#FFFBEB`, or `#EFF6FF`).
- Contains a clear title in `headline-sm`, advisory body text in `body-md`, and an inline action slot for direct response controls.

### Tactical Lists & Telemetry Tables
- Row height: 44px fixed for dense telemetry, 56px for citizen logs.
- Hairline dividers (`#E2E8F0`), zero zebra striping. Header row uses `#F8FAFC` background with `label-badge` styling. Hover states apply `#F8FAFC` background. Coordinates and resource tallies format strictly in `data-mono-md`.
